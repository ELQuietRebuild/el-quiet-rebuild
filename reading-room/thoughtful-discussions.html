Not every question needs an immediate answer. Thoughtful Discussions is a space to examine an idea, question what we assume and think more carefully about the choices shaping who we become.

The purpose is not to tell you what to think. It is to give you something worth thinking about.

Should Your Emotions Decide What You Do Next?

A thoughtful discussion about emotion, responsibility and the person you are becoming.

We cannot always control what we feel. But should every emotion be allowed to decide what happens next?

There are moments when we feel hurt, angry, embarrassed or disappointed. Those emotions are real. But feeling something and allowing that feeling to determine our next action are not necessarily the same thing.

What happens when the emotion of the moment wants one thing, but the person you are becoming may need something different?
