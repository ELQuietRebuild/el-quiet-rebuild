EL QUIET READING ROOM

Quiet Reading Sessions

You don't have to read alone.

EL Quiet Reading Sessions are calm, welcoming spaces created to help you make time for reading, become more consistent and enjoy the experience alongside others.

We read quietly together, slow down from everyday distractions, and make space for gentle conversation, reflection and questions along the way.

THE EXPERIENCE

What Happens in a Quiet Reading Session?

Nothing complicated. Bring the book you're currently reading, find a comfortable spot and settle in with us.

We spend time reading quietly, pause for a little conversation, share reflections or questions, and then return to our books — sometimes with calm music creating a gentle reading atmosphere in the background.

READ WITH CONSISTENCY

For the Book You Keep Meaning to Read

Sometimes the hardest part of reading is simply making time to begin — or returning to a book after putting it down.

EL Quiet Reading Sessions offer a little accountability and company to help you show up, open your book and keep going. You don't need to be a fast reader, and you don't need to be reading the same book as everyone else. Just bring what you're reading and join us.
