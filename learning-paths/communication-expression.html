Communication & Expression

I know what I mean, but I struggle to explain myself clearly.

What might be making communication difficult?

Communication can become difficult for different reasons. You may know what you want to say but struggle to organize your thoughts, find the right words under pressure, or explain your point in a way another person can understand.

Clear communication is something you can develop through knowledge, structure, practice, and experience.

What can you learn and practise?

  • Organize your thoughts before you speak or write.
  • Answer questions with a clear structure.
  • Explain your reasoning so others can follow your thinking.
  • Express disagreement clearly and respectfully.
  • Communicate more effectively in professional situations.

Try this

Choose one simple question and answer it using this structure:

  1. Main point
  2. Reason
  3. Example
  4. Conclusion

Example question: What is one skill you want to improve, and why does it matter to you?

Write your answer in four parts: make your main point, explain your reason, give an example, and finish with a clear conclusion.
